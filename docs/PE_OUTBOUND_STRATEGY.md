# PE Outbound Strategy: Fortium Fractional Leadership

> Based on PitchBook Q4 2025 "Balancing Act" Report Analysis
> Created: 2026-01-12

## Core Thesis

**PE needs execution capacity, not capital.** The market is thawing but constrained—deal backlogs clearing, portfolios aging, distributions weak. Fortium delivers world-class CIO/CTO/CISO leadership fractionally when PE firms need horsepower without headcount.

---

## Market Context (Why Now)

| Signal | Data Point | Fortium Implication |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|
| Deal acceleration | Above trend since 2022 | More integrations, carveouts = leadership gaps |
| Portfolio aging | 30% held >5 years (highest since 2011) | Execution fatigue, need fresh capability |
| Weak distributions | DPI below historical norms | Must generate realized value faster |
| Credit stress | Downgrades > upgrades, PIK rising | Capital discipline = execution quality matters more |
| Manager alpha | 35-64 bps from better selection | Leader selection = same alpha inside portcos |

## 7 Key Messages for PE Buyers

### Message 1: Execution Over Capital
> "The PE environment is no longer about access to capital—it's about access to execution capacity."

*Cite: Deal backlog clearing, activity above trend*

### Message 2: Leadership Horsepower
> "Your biggest constraint in 2025 is not money. It's leadership horsepower."

*Cite: Portfolio aging + weak DPI = execute more, faster, fewer resources*

### Message 3: Big Talent, Small Cost
> "Fractional leadership gets you big-company talent at small-company cost."

*Cite: Borrowing compressed but quality concerns rising—avoid permanent G&A*

### Message 4: No Room for Mediocrity
> "You cannot afford mediocre leadership during holding periods that stretch 5-7 years."

*Cite: Portfolio aging is a crisis hidden in plain sight*

### Message 5: Selection = Alpha
> "Good manager selection creates alpha. Good leadership selection creates enterprise value."

*Cite: 35-64 bps from selection skill—same applies to tech leaders*

### Message 6: Access Elite Talent
> "Fortium is the only way to access CTO/CIO/CISO leaders who would never join your $100M-$1B portfolio company full-time."

*Angle: Unlock talent otherwise inaccessible to middle market*

### Message 7: Execute Under Constraint
> "Winners in 2025-2026 will be those who execute transformations under resource constraint."

*Cite: Tariffs, cost pressures, wage compression, energy volatility*
